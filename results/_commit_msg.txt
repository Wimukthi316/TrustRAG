Serve a real corpus record as the demo, and rebuild the result view

The demo highlighted the entire answer. The first explanation, that C1
over-flags, was wrong. Measured over 146 RAGTruth test responses through the
serving path, C1 marks a median of 0.4% of the answer and never once marked
the whole thing, and its offsets land within a character or two of gold. The
whole-answer highlight was the hand-written example being out of distribution:
a 264-character context against RAGTruth's thousand-plus.

/api/example now takes a name and defaults to ragtruth_qa, test record 16121
kept verbatim with a test asserting the offsets still slice correctly. The
hand-written example stays under the name handwritten and is labelled as
invented, because showing the failure beside the working case is more useful
than removing it. Record 16121 was picked by measurement: of 37 candidates it
was the only one whose decisions change as alpha moves, so the conformal layer
can be demonstrated live. No threshold was touched.

Result view rebuilt against the running app:

- dropped the "Supported" badge, which counted PASS spans that the backend
  never returns and so permanently read zero
- added a "% of the answer marked" badge, an empty state, and a legend that
  explains the decisions in terms of the prediction set
- span detail pins on click; score labels now say what the probability is of
- task-type selector, instead of sending "other" for everything
- stopped CSS-uppercasing the Greek alpha, which rendered as a capital A

run_demo.ps1 takes -Detector c1|lettucedetect and defaults to c1; it had been
starting the baseline.

Also corrects a claim in the detector docstring. RAGTruth gold spans are not
usually whole sentences: median 35 characters, 11.1% exactly one sentence,
median response has 7% of its answer marked. Span merging is a real cost.
